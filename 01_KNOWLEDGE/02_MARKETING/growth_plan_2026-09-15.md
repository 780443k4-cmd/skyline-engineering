# Growth Plan — 60 Days (2026-09-15 → 2026-11-13)

**Owner:** AGT-02 CONTENT_PROOF · drafted for Denis · status: DRAFT, awaiting Denis review before publication

## Diagnosis

All three channels are flat on followers despite steady publishing: Instagram 258/25 posts
(258/24 two weeks ago), Facebook 145 (unchanged), TikTok 1 (unchanged) — even though
individual TikToks pull 200–800 real views. The Instagram format data explains why: over the
last 30 days, Reels are 80.2% of views but only 25% of engagement; carousels/static posts are
19.8% of views but 75% of engagement. Reels earn passive reach — people watch and scroll on.
Carousels earn the signals (saves, comments, profile visits) that correlate with hitting
Follow. We've been optimizing for a number (views) that doesn't move the number we're
accountable for (followers). TikTok shows the same pattern, more extreme: real views, ~0%
conversion to follows.

## What changes

**Instagram** — flip the mix. Going forward, roughly two of every three posts are
carousel/static, not Reels. Reels stay in rotation for reach and for topics that need motion
(process, site footage), but they are no longer the default format.

**Facebook** — Facebook's real lever isn't page growth, it's the one-time manual distribution
to the 9 relevant groups on each post. Keep doing that every time; a Facebook post that only
goes to the page isn't finished.

**TikTok — recommendation, not a decision, pending Denis's sign-off:** reduce TikTok to
repurposed vertical cuts of the same IG Reels instead of separate production. This does not
resolve the open question of *why* TikTok views don't convert — that stays unresolved — it's a
capacity reallocation, freeing production time for the IG/FB formats that are actually moving
followers, until Denis decides otherwise.

record of how any past post performed, which is why this review had to be reconstructed live
instead of read. Starting with the first post in this calendar, every publication gets a
performance entry (platform, format, publish date, 7-day views/engagement, follower count at
publish and +7 days) logged in `05_SOCIAL/performance/`, mirrored in `08_AUDIT/publications/`
per the existing release-gate rule. Without this, the next review is blind again.
